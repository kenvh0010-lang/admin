Everyone knows the eighty-twenty rule, yet almost nobody runs the math a second time. When you do, a hidden second layer appears.

Source: Vilfredo Pareto, Cours d'economie politique (1896)

Vilfredo Pareto's 1896 Land Discovery

In 1896, Italian economist Vilfredo Pareto showed that about twenty percent of the people in Italy owned about eighty percent of the land. The popular story about pea pods in his garden is a legend.

  • He measured land ownership across Italy
  • The pea-pod garden story is a popular myth

Source: Vilfredo Pareto, Cours d'economie politique (1896)

Apply 80/20 to the Top 20% Again

Marketing writer Perry Marshall points out that if the pattern holds, Pareto repeats inside itself. Take twenty percent of twenty percent and eighty percent of eighty percent: just four percent of your actions could create sixty-four percent of results.

Source: Perry Marshall, 80/20 Sales and Marketing (2013)

Three Passes = 51% From Under 1%

Source: Math: 0.2^3 = 0.8% of inputs, 0.8^3 = 51% of results, if the 80/20 pattern holds
