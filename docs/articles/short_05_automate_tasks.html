You Are Acting Like a Human Router

A 2012 McKinsey report estimated that knowledge workers spend about twenty-eight percent of their workweek just reading and answering email.

  • McKinsey (2012): ~28% of the workweek goes to email
  • Copying data across tabs drains deep creative focus

Source: McKinsey Global Institute, 'The social economy' (July 2012)

The Engineer's 3-Strike Automation Rule

Programmers have a rule of three: the third time you repeat something, automate it. Apply it to your week. If you do the same digital task three times, stop and automate it.

  • 1st time: Do it manually to learn the steps
  • 3rd time in a week: Build a permanent AI or webhook flow

Source: 'Rule of three', popularized in Martin Fowler, Refactoring (1999)

3 Chores to Hand Off Immediately
