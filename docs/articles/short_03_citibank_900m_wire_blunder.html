Two Unchecked Boxes Made Citibank Wire $900 Million by Mistake

On August 11, 2020, Citibank intended to wire $7.8 million in Revlon interest but accidentally wired nearly $900 million of its own funds after missing two Flexcube software checkboxes. By 2026, erroneous-payment clauses appear in 88% of corporate credit agreements.

Meant $7.8M. Wired $900M.

Citibank meant to wire 7.8 million dollars in interest for Revlon. Instead, it accidentally wired almost 900 million of its own money.

Source: In re Citibank Aug. 11, 2020 Wire Transfers, Opinion p. 2 (S.D.N.Y. 2021)
