B.F. Skinner's Variable Ratio Discovery

In 1957, Harvard psychologist B.F. Skinner showed that when food dropped on a predictable schedule, pigeons stayed calm. When payouts turned random, they pressed the lever obsessively.

  • Fixed pellets: Pigeons only pressed the lever when hungry
  • Random pellets: Pigeons pecked nonstop until exhaustion

Uncertainty Spikes Dopamine Higher Than Winning

Flash-sale countdowns, mystery discounts, and pull-to-refresh feeds exploit that exact uncertainty spike. Your brain chases the anticipation of a deal, not the item itself.
