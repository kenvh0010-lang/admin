103 ICUs Hit a Median Infection Rate of Zero

Tracking 103 Michigan ICUs across 1,981 ICU-months, Pronovost introduced five basic central-line steps and gave nurses full authority to stop any doctor who skipped one. Within three months, the median infection rate dropped to zero.

  • 5 basic steps: handwash, drapes, skin prep, vein site, removal
