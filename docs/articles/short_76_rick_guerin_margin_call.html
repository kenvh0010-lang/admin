Why Buffett and Munger's Third Partner Vanished from the Billionaire List

Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger originally invested alongside a brilliant third partner named Rick Guerin. During the 1973-1974 market crash (~70% drop), Guerin was leveraged on margin loans and forced to sell his Berkshire Hathaway shares to Buffett for under $40 apiece.

Buffett and Munger Originally Had a Third Partner

Everyone studies Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger, yet almost nobody remembers their brilliant third investing partner, Rick Guerin, who sold his Berkshire Hathaway shares for under forty dollars apiece.

  • Invested side by side with Buffett & Munger in the 1960s-70s
  • Forced out just before decades of historic compounding

Source: Warren Buffett interview recounted by Mohnish Pabrai (2013)

Margin Loans Met the 1973-1974 70% Bear Crash

During the brutal 1973 and 1974 market crash, when stocks plunged roughly seventy percent, Guerin held margin loans and got hit with margin calls, forcing him to sell his Berkshire stock to Buffett under forty dollars.

  • Guerin borrowed margin debt to accelerate his returns
  • Lenders forced him to sell Berkshire shares to Buffett under $40

Source: Warren Buffett interview recounted by Mohnish Pabrai (2013)

Equally Smart, Only Impatient With Borrowed Debt

Years later, Buffett told investor Mohnish Pabrai why Rick disappeared from the billionaire ranks: Charlie and Warren knew they would grow wealthy eventually, whereas equally smart Rick was in a hurry.

Source: Warren Buffett interview recounted by Mohnish Pabrai (2013)

Never Carry Callable Debt Into a Multi-Year Slump

Ban callable margin debt from your core portfolio and hold twelve months of living cash in reserve, ensuring no bear market ever forces you to liquidate decades of future compounding.

Source: Mind & Margin protocol (advice, not a study)

You Only Need to Get Rich Once

You only have to get rich once, so never gamble permanent survival just to cross the finish line two years early.

  • Patience survives every 50% to 70% historical drawdown
  • Leverage hands strangers the right to sell your bottom
