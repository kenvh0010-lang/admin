Bill Perkins' 'Memory Dividends' Rule Changed How I View Money

Automated saving builds security, but autopilot hoarding past your target misses biological windows money can never reopen. Here is Bill Perkins' Memory Dividends mental model from Die With Zero.

Peak Net Worth at Peak Frailty?

What is the point of reaching your highest net worth at age seventy-five if you skipped every decade your body had the energy to enjoy it?

  • Millions defer every meaningful trip to an abstract future
  • Money compounds forever, but physical energy has seasons

Bill Perkins & Memory Dividends

In his book Die With Zero, Bill Perkins introduces a mental model called Memory Dividends. Just as index funds pay cash dividends, meaningful experiences pay emotional dividends every time you revisit them.

  • Financial assets pay cash dividends into your account
  • Great experiences pay memory dividends every year you live

Autopilot Hoarding vs Biological Windows

Over-hoarding past your automated investing target ignores biological windows. Taking a demanding mountain hike at thirty or traveling with aging parents carries an expiration date money cannot reopen.

The 5-Year Time-Bucket Protocol

Apply the Time-Bucket Rule: automate your baseline wealth investments on payday first, then intentionally fund three experiences over the next five years that belong to your current health window.

Balance Your Future Floor and Present Life

Smart wealth planning optimizes fulfillment across decades, not just a final bank balance.

  • Never raid your automated compounding base
  • Never let fear make you miss non-renewable life seasons
