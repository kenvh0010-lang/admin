Why Going 10x Is Psychologically Easier Than Going 2x

Why 2x Ambition Breeds Exhaustion

Aiming for ten-times growth sounds ten times harder than doubling your current output. Psychologically, the exact opposite is true.

  • Most people assume bigger targets require 10x more hours
  • In practice, moderate targets trap you inside broken routines

Source: Dan Sullivan & Benjamin Hardy, 10x Is Easier Than 2x (2023)

Sullivan & Hardy's 10x Filter

Strategist Dan Sullivan and psychologist Benjamin Hardy explain why in 10x Is Easier Than 2x. Chasing two-x growth tricks you into keeping your current habits and simply grinding twice as long.

  • You can brute-force 2x growth by sacrificing sleep and weekends
  • Brute force never forces you to question low-value habits

Source: Dan Sullivan & Benjamin Hardy, 10x Is Easier Than 2x (2023)

Linear Brute Force vs Asymmetric Pruning

A two-x target lets eighty percent of your daily busywork survive. A ten-x target makes brute force impossible, forcing you to drop everything except the twenty percent asymmetric core.

Source: Dan Sullivan & Benjamin Hardy, 10x Is Easier Than 2x (2023)
