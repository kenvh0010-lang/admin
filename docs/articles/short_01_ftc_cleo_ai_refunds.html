2.1 Million Cleo AI Users Are Getting FTC Refunds via PayPal

Starting October 27, 2026, the Federal Trade Commission began sending 2,124,796 PayPal payments totaling more than $15.8 million to users of the cash-advance app Cleo AI, following a $17 million 2025 settlement order in federal court.

2.1 Million PayPal Refunds Went Out

Over 2.1 million people are getting PayPal refunds after using an AI cash-advance app. Total payout: more than 15.8 million dollars.

Source: FTC Refund Notice (Oct 8 & Oct 27, 2026)

Promised Up to $250 Today

The FTC says Cleo's ads promised up to 250 or 500 dollars today, if users paid for a monthly subscription.

Source: FTC v. Cleo AI, No. 1:25-cv-02594, Complaint p. 5 (S.D.N.Y.)

Almost Nobody Got $250

The FTC alleged only 0.3 percent of Plus borrowers got the full 250 dollars, and paid express transfers could still arrive the next day.

Source: FTC v. Cleo AI, Complaint (S.D.N.Y. filed Mar/May 2025)

Blocked From Canceling

It also alleged users with an unpaid advance could not cancel. Cleo settled for 17 million dollars, and no court ruled on the claims.

Source: FTC Complaint p. 15; Stipulated Order (2025)

Count Fees Like Interest

When a small advance requires a subscription plus a rush fee, treat both fees as interest before you tap accept.

Source: FTC Consumer Refund Program (Oct 2026)
