Eminem and Elvis vs. the $5.4 Billion AI Song App

On November 6, 2026, Eminem's publisher Eight Mile Style and the owners of Elvis Presley's catalog filed separate lawsuits against Suno, an AI music app valued at $5.4 billion. These are allegations; no court has ruled.

Eminem's Publisher Wants $41 Million

Eminem's publisher wants 41 million dollars from an AI app.

Source: Detroit Free Press, Nov 7, 2026

Then Elvis Sued Too

The same night, Elvis Presley's catalog owners sued too.

Source: Variety, Nov 7, 2026

Trained on Almost Every Song Online

The lawsuit quotes Suno's own words: its AI learned from essentially all music of reasonable quality on the open internet.

Source: Eight Mile Style v. Suno, No. 3:26-cv-13399 (N.D. Cal.)

A $5.4 Billion Company

That music helped build a 5.4 billion dollar company. The songwriters got nothing, the suit says.

Source: Suno blog, Jun 3, 2026; complaint via Detroit Free Press, Nov 7, 2026

Owners Set the Price

No court has ruled yet. But the rule is old: whoever owns the asset sets the price, even against a billion dollar machine.

Source: Suno statement to Variety, Nov 7, 2026
