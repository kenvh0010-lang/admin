12% vs 99%: The Power of One Checkbox

Why did Germany have a twelve-percent organ donor consent rate while neighboring Austria hit ninety-nine percent? One tiny default checkbox.

  • Nearly identical culture, education, and values
  • One form design difference changed behavior 8x

Johnson & Goldstein's 2003 'Science' Study

In a 2003 Science paper, psychologists Eric Johnson and Dan Goldstein showed that people rarely change pre-selected options. Germany required citizens to opt in to organ donation; Austria made it opt-out.

  • Germany required checking a box to join (opt-in)
  • Austria enrolled citizens unless they opted out
