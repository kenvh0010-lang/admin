CFPB Ordered Cash App Parent Block to Pay Up to $175 Million

In Consent Order 2025-CFPB-0001, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau ordered Cash App parent Block, Inc. to pay up to $120 million in consumer redress plus a $55 million civil penalty over fraud-investigation and customer-support failures. Block consented without admitting or denying the findings.

Up to $175M CFPB Order on Block

The CFPB ordered Cash App's parent company, Block, to pay up to 175 million dollars over how it handled fraud on the app.

Source: In the Matter of Block, Inc., 2025-CFPB-0001, p. 1 (Jan 16, 2025)

Card Number Played a Recording

Until 2021, the number on the back of Cash Cards played a recording with no live agent, pushing victims toward fake support numbers online.

Source: CFPB Consent Order Para. 7-9, p. 12 (2025-CFPB-0001)

Fought 75% of Bank Chargebacks

When users asked their banks to reverse unauthorized peer-to-peer transfers, the CFPB found Block challenged at least 75 percent of those chargebacks.

Source: CFPB Consent Order Para. 44-45, p. 24 (2025-CFPB-0001)

Up to $120M Back to Users

Without admitting or denying the findings, Block agreed to up to 120 million dollars in user redress, a 55 million penalty, and live phone support.

Source: CFPB Consent Order Para. 117 & 126, pp. 56, 61

Apps Are Wallets, Not Vaults

Use payment apps like cash in your pocket, not a vault. Sweep idle balances straight back to an insured bank account.

Source: CFPB Consent Order 2025-CFPB-0001
